# Expanded Nursing Organizational Structure

## 1. Director of Nursing

The Director of Nursing (DON) is accountable for the overall governance, strategic direction, clinical performance, workforce planning, quality, safety, and operational management of nursing services.

### Primary Responsibilities

- Nursing strategy and governance
- Nursing workforce capacity and productivity
- Patient-care standards
- Nursing quality and patient safety
- Staffing and skill-mix oversight
- Nursing budget and resource utilization
- Regulatory and accreditation compliance
- Nursing performance management
- Workforce system governance

## 2. Deputy / Assistant Director of Nursing

Supports the DON in executing nursing strategy and overseeing day-to-day nursing operations.

### Responsibilities

- Operational nursing oversight
- Unit performance
- Staffing escalation
- Workforce deployment
- Clinical coordination
- Service continuity
- Nursing manager supervision

## 3. Nursing Operations

Responsible for direct nursing service delivery across clinical units.

### Clinical Areas

- Medical Nursing
- Surgical Nursing
- Critical Care / ICU
- Emergency Department
- Operating Room
- Pediatrics
- Maternity / Obstetrics
- Specialty Units
- Other inpatient and outpatient nursing services

## 4. Nursing Administration / Workforce Management

Responsible for the complete nursing workforce lifecycle.

### Functions

- Manpower planning
- Recruitment and hiring coordination
- Staff Nurse Master Data
- License and credential verification
- Position and vacancy management
- Staff allocation
- Shift scheduling
- Attendance
- Leave
- Overtime
- Contract renewal
- Retention monitoring
- Workforce analytics

## 5. Nursing Education & Development

Responsible for maintaining nursing competency and professional development.

### Functions

- Orientation
- New-hire onboarding
- Competency assessment
- Mandatory training
- Continuing education
- Clinical education
- Career development
- Training compliance

## 6. Nursing Quality & Patient Safety

Responsible for nursing quality, patient safety, compliance, and continuous improvement.

### Functions

- Nursing audits
- Quality indicators
- Patient safety
- Incident management
- Clinical standards
- Regulatory compliance
- Accreditation readiness
- Performance improvement

## 7. Nursing Informatics

Responsible for nursing information systems, data, analytics, and digital transformation.

### Functions

- EMR integration
- Nursing systems
- Workforce system administration
- Nursing dashboards
- Data governance
- Nursing analytics
- Clinical/workforce interoperability

        │

### Workforce System Governance Principle

**Staff Nurse Master Data** should provide the authoritative workforce identity and employment profile used across nursing workforce processes.

The system should integrate with:

- HR
- EMR
- Time & Attendance

- Finance
- Learning / Training
- Credentialing
- Identity / Access Management

## Enterprise Integration Matrix

| System / Department | Direction | Workforce Management | Primary Data |
|---|---|---|---|
| EMR | Bidirectional / event-based | Workforce System | Census, ADT, acuity, clinical demand |
| HR | Bidirectional | Workforce System | Employee, position, employment status |
| Credentialing | Bidirectional | Workforce System | License, registration, credentials |
| Scheduling | Internal / integrated | Workforce System | Roster, shifts, assignments |

| Finance / ERP | Bidirectional | Workforce System | Cost centers, labor cost, financial posting |
| Learning Management | Bidirectional | Workforce System | Training and competency |

# Final Organizational Model

The recommended structure establishes a clear hierarchy:

**Hospital Director → Director of Nursing → Nursing Management → Charge Nurse → Staff Nurse → Patient Care**

while the technology architecture establishes the operational flow:

**EMR → Clinical Demand → Workforce Planning → Staff Nurse Master Data → Credentialing → Scheduling → Attendance → Leave/Overtime → Payroll → Finance → Analytics**

This creates a single, governed operating model connecting **hospital leadership, clinical operations, nursing workforce management, HR, payroll, finance, EMR, and enterprise analytics**.
